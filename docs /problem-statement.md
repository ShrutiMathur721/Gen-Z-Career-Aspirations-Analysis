# Problem Statement: Understanding Gen Z Career Aspirations

## Background

Gen Z is entering the workforce with clear expectations around salary, career growth, learning opportunities, flexibility, work-life balance, job security, and meaningful work.

At the same time, employers are trying to understand how to attract, manage, and retain young talent while also meeting business and skill requirements.

This creates a possible gap between:

- What Gen Z expects from a career
- What employers expect from Gen Z employees
- What workplaces actually provide

The purpose of this project is to understand that gap using survey data, Power BI analysis, and employer-review insights.

---

## Problem

Many Gen Z students, fresh graduates, job seekers, and early-career professionals are still trying to understand which careers and workplace environments suit them best.

Common concerns include:

- Career growth
- Learning and skill development
- Salary expectations
- Work-life balance
- Flexible or hybrid work
- Manager support
- Job security
- Meaningful work
- Company mission and values

If these expectations are not understood properly, Gen Z employees may struggle to find suitable opportunities or may leave organizations when their expectations are not met.

Employers may also face challenges in attracting, engaging, and retaining young employees.

---

## Objective

The main objective of this project is to understand:

> **What does Gen Z expect from employers, managers, and the workplace, and where might those expectations differ from real employer experiences?**

The analysis focuses mainly on:

- Manager expectations
- Preferred work environment
- Career growth
- Learning opportunities
- Salary expectations
- Long-term retention
- Company mission
- Social impact
- Workplace satisfaction

---

## 5W1H Framework

### What?

Analyse Gen Z career aspirations and understand the factors that influence their career and employer choices.

### Why?

A mismatch between employee expectations and workplace reality can contribute to:

- Early attrition
- Low engagement
- Poor employer-employee fit
- Recruitment challenges
- Difficulty retaining young talent

### Who?

The project focuses on:

- Gen Z students
- Fresh graduates
- Job seekers
- Early-career professionals

Employer perspectives are also considered through employee reviews and workplace observations.

### Where?

The analysis mainly focuses on the **Indian job market**, including:

- Corporate organizations
- Start-ups
- Traditional office workplaces
- Hybrid and flexible workplaces

### When?

The problem is most visible when Gen Z:

- Moves from education into employment
- Searches for a first job
- Starts an early-career role
- Considers changing jobs
- Participates in campus recruitment

### How?

The project uses:

- Gen Z survey data
- Data cleaning and preparation
- Power BI dashboards
- DAX measures and KPIs
- Employee-review analysis
- Comparison of Gen Z expectations with employer experience

---

## Key Business Question

> **How can employers better attract and retain Gen Z by understanding their expectations around managers, growth, flexibility, compensation, and company purpose?**

---

## Expected Outcome

The goal is to convert Gen Z survey responses into practical insights that can help:

- Recruiters improve employer messaging
- HR teams understand retention risks
- Managers better understand early-career expectations
- Employers identify gaps between employee expectations and workplace experience

The project aims to move beyond general assumptions about Gen Z and use data to identify which workplace factors matter most.
